# Act 2 (the Hive): design

Date: 2026-11-06. Requested by the user: "build the sim into act 2". They skipped the design questions, so every call here is mine and can be reversed.

## Why

The agent can clear a real Act 1. The next floor of the game is out of the sim's reach.

- **Success:** a sim run continues through Act 2's boss, the fights match the live game under `sim.validate`, and a model trained on it plays Act 2 in the live game.
- **Failure:** a monster quietly differs from the game, or Act 1 behaviour changes (a model must keep its Act 1 results after growing).

## Order

1. Hive fights in `sim/engine.py`, validated live. Nothing trains yet.
2. The run: act change, boss rewards, Hive map, events and Ancients.
3. `live.py` continues past the Act 1 boss.
4. Grow the models and train, after the prompt-cards queue finishes.

## Not in scope, calls made without the user

- Card upgrades. They matter more in Act 2, but they're their own project and also change rest sites. The agent will be weaker here than a human.
- Act 3.
- Relics at the act change. (Corrected 2026-11-09: STS2 has no boss relics. The Act 1 boss pays gold, a potion roll and an all-Rare card offer, and the relic comes from the Act 2 Ancient instead. The sim heals to full there and skips its relic offer. Live play picks with the Neow rule.)
- Hive events and Ancients start as the live rule (leave if allowed, else first safe option) and as empty rooms in the sim.

## Results (2026-11-09)

**A sim run now goes through Act 2, but the agent almost never clears it: 2.2% of runs, against 0.4% before training.** Act 1 held within noise. Most Act 2 deaths moved from regular fights to the boss.

### Fights against the live game

`sim.validate` on all 26 Hive monsters: 34 of 1,185 steps differed on the first pass, across all 20 encounters. A second pass on Bowlbugs, Ovicopter and two bosses differed on 1 of 238 steps: Tough Egg's Hatch counter (sim 2, live 0) and its HP. A staged live run (`agent/live_act2_check.py`, Act 1 models) killed Vantom with debug commands, entered the Ancient (Pael) and played Act 2 for real until it died on floor 8. The trained Act 2 models haven't played the live game yet.

### Training

Run by `.superpowers/queue/act2.sh` from the frozen `auto-spire-act2t` worktree, starting from v14 + v7 grown with `--insert-cards`.

| Stage | What | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| A | combat on Hive and Act 1 encounters, random decks, prompt practice | 30M |
| B | combat inside whole runs, with the grown run policy | 30M |
| R | run policy with event practice, on B's combat model | 6M |

Phase B crashed once, an hour in: a Thieving Hopper stole a generated card that wasn't in the deck. The game never does that (a generated card has no `DeckVersion`), so the sim was fixed at 1763212 and B restarted from A's final model.
